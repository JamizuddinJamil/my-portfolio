A few things people usually ask.

How fast can you start?

Usually within a week of agreeing on scope, depending on current workload , I'll give you a real date once I see the project.

Do you require full payment upfront?

No , typically a deposit to begin, with the remainder due on delivery. Exact terms are agreed in the quote.

Can you work with an existing design?

Yes , if you already have a Figma design or brand guidelines, I can build directly from those instead of starting from scratch.

What do you need from me to get started?

A rough idea of what the site/app needs to do, any existing branding, and your content (text/images) , or I can help figure out placeholders if it isn't ready yet.
