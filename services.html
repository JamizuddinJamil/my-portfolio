Landing pages

Built to convert one visitor into one action.

A landing page has one job: get the visitor to do the one thing you need , book a call, sign up, or buy. No distracting navigation, no competing messages.

  • ✓ One clear call-to-action, above and below the fold
  • ✓ Mobile-first , most of your traffic reads it on a phone
  • ✓ Fast load time, optimized images, no bloated scripts
  • ✓ Form or booking link connected and tested before handover
Company profile websites

The site people check before they trust you.

When someone searches your business name, this is what they find. It needs to look legitimate, explain what you do, and make it easy to get in touch.

  • ✓ Home, About, Services, and Contact pages as standard
  • ✓ Structured so you can update text/images yourself later
  • ✓ Basic on-page SEO so you can be found on Google
  • ✓ Contact form and business details clearly placed
